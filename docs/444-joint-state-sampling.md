# Native 4×4 joint-state sampling: coordinate construction prototype

## Purpose

This milestone adds explicit facelet writers for the corner, centre and wing coordinates. It makes the coordinate product concrete and testable before claiming legal reachability or uniform random-state generation.

## Coordinate definitions

- **Corners:** a permutation of eight corner identities and seven independent twist values; the final twist makes the twist sum zero modulo three.
- **Centres:** a uniformly shuffled multiset containing four each of U, R, F, D, L and B.
- **Wings:** a permutation of 24 labelled wing identities. The identity-to-slot sticker mapping is derived by propagating each solved wing through the repository's independent facelet move permutations. No independent wing-orientation bit is sampled.

The wing mapping is guarded: if two legal move paths put the same labelled wing in the same slot with different sticker assignments, initialisation throws instead of silently assuming the permutation-only model.

## What the tests establish

The facelet-construction tests combine independently sampled coordinates and verify 16 stickers of each colour, corner-coordinate round trips, centre-colour round trips, valid wing-pair counts, and preservation of facelets outside the coordinate being written.

These tests establish representation consistency only. They do not prove that the combined state is reachable by legal 4×4 turns.

## What remains unproven

The independent coordinate product is **not** a production random-state sampler. We have not yet proved that every generated combined state is reachable, or that every reachable visible state has equal probability after accounting for equivalences and constraints.

The next milestone must derive the move-group constraints for these coordinates and validate the derivation against states generated by legal move sequences. The production generator and dependencies remain unchanged until reachability and solving are verified.
