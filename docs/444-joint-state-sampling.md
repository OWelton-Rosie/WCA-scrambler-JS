# Native 4×4 joint-state sampling: coordinate construction prototype

## Purpose

This milestone adds explicit facelet writers for corner, centre and wing coordinates. It makes the coordinate product concrete and testable before claiming legal reachability or uniform random-state generation.

## Coordinate definitions

- **Corners:** a permutation of eight corner identities and seven independent twist values; the final twist makes the twist sum zero modulo three.
- **Centres:** a uniformly shuffled multiset containing four each of U, R, F, D, L and B.
- **Wings:** 24 geometric wing slots, each assigned one of the 12 adjacent face-colour pairs exactly twice, plus one visible sticker-order bit per slot.

The centre and wing writers preserve facelets outside their respective piece classes. The corner writer has the same property.

## What the tests establish

The facelet-construction tests combine independently sampled coordinates and verify 16 stickers of each colour, corner round trips, centre-colour round trips, two wings of each edge-colour pair, and preservation of facelets outside the coordinate being written.

These tests establish representation consistency only. They do not prove that the combined state is reachable by legal 4×4 turns.

## What remains unproven

The independent coordinate product is **not** a production random-state sampler. We have not yet proved that every generated combined state is reachable, or that every reachable visible state has equal probability after accounting for equivalences and constraints.

The next milestone must derive the move-group constraints for these visible coordinates and validate the derivation against states generated by legal move sequences. The production generator and dependencies remain unchanged until reachability and solving are verified.
