# 4×4 coordinate model: observable invariants and open constraints

This note records what the current facelet model can verify. It intentionally does **not** claim a complete state-space factorisation or a uniform full-state sampler.

## Observable coordinate sizes

The geometric facelet model groups the 96 stickers into:

- 8 corners, each with three stickers;
- 24 wing locations, each with two stickers;
- 24 centre locations, each with one sticker.

The solved facelet state has 16 stickers of each colour. Under every supported legal move, the following observable conditions should remain true:

1. The eight corner colour triples are the eight legal corner identities, each appearing once; their orientation coordinate obeys the corner twist constraint.
2. The 24 centre positions contain exactly four stickers of each colour.
3. Each of the 12 unordered edge-colour pairs occurs at exactly two wing locations.

These are necessary checks for states produced by legal moves. They are not a complete reachability test: passing them does not prove that an arbitrary facelet assignment can be reached from solved.

## Why independently sampled marginals are not enough

The current corner, centre and wing sampler modules sample three separate marginals:

- corner permutation and twist coordinate;
- centre-colour multiset arrangement;
- a permutation of 24 abstract wing labels.

They do not yet define a joint distribution over reachable facelet states. In particular:

- a wing permutation is not yet a sticker assignment; the two stickers and their orientation must be mapped consistently;
- two wings with the same edge-colour pair are visually indistinguishable, so an abstract labelling must not accidentally bias visible states;
- centres are represented by colours, not individually observable identities;
- any joint parity/reachability conditions must be derived for this representation rather than assumed from the 3×3 cube;
- the corner, wing and centre coordinates must be combined and round-tripped through the independent facelet move model.

For those reasons, no product of coordinate counts is recorded here as the 4×4 state-space size. A count is useful only after the coordinate mapping, equivalence classes and constraints have been justified.

## Validation added

`src/test/444/coordinate-invariants.test.js` checks the observable conditions against every currently supported single-move notation and a long deterministic sequence of legal moves. This test is a regression guard for the coordinate extractors and move model, not a proof of uniformity or full reachability.

## Required evidence before implementing the joint sampler

1. Define the exact abstract identity and orientation of every wing and its mapping to two facelets.
2. Prove how duplicate wing-colour pairs are quotiented into observable states, including any orientation distinctions.
3. Derive all necessary and sufficient reachability constraints for the chosen coordinates, including interactions among corners, wings and centres.
4. Independently validate the derivation against states generated by legal moves and, where feasible, exhaustive smaller abstractions.
5. Only then implement the joint sampler and document why each reachable visible state has the same probability.

Until these steps are complete, the existing production generator must remain unchanged.
